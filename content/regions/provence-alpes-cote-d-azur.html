Provence-Alpes-Côte d'Azur

6 départements

Provence-Alpes-Côte d'Azur is located in southeastern France, along the Mediterranean coast. As of 2021 it counted 5,127,840 inhabitants (the 7th most populous French région), across 31,676 km² of land (the 9th largest by area). Its GDP stood at €217,942 million in 2024 (provisional), ranking it 5th nationally. Its largest cities are Marseille, Nice and Toulon.

87 496 rooftop PV installations totalling 1430.7 MWp have been identified across Provence-Alpes-Côte d'Azur by OpenPVMapper — the 4th highest installed capacity among French régions. Bouches-du-Rhône, Var and Alpes-de-Haute-Provence lead the région's départements by capacity. These detections span 2017–2025. Click here to see it on the map.
