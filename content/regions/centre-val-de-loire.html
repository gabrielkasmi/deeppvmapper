Centre-Val de Loire

6 départements

Centre-Val de Loire is located in the center of France. As of 2021 it counted 2,573,303 inhabitants (the 12th most populous French région), across 39,507 km² of land (the 6th largest by area). Its GDP stood at €91,467 million in 2024 (provisional), ranking it 12th nationally. Its largest cities are Tours, Orléans and Bourges.

41 485 rooftop PV installations totalling 668.9 MWp have been identified across Centre-Val de Loire by OpenPVMapper — the 8th highest installed capacity among French régions. Indre-et-Loire, Cher and Loir-et-Cher lead the région's départements by capacity. These detections span 2018–2025. Click here to see it on the map.
