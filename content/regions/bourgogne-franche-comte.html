Bourgogne-Franche-Comté

8 départements

Bourgogne-Franche-Comté is located in eastern-central France. As of 2021 it counted 2,800,194 inhabitants (the 11th most populous French région), across 48,036 km² of land (the 5th largest by area). Its GDP stood at €95,775 million in 2024 (provisional), ranking it 11th nationally. Its largest cities are Dijon, Besançon and Belfort.

50 719 rooftop PV installations totalling 756.6 MWp have been identified across Bourgogne-Franche-Comté by OpenPVMapper — the 7th highest installed capacity among French régions. Yonne, Saône-et-Loire and Jura lead the région's départements by capacity. These detections span 2020–2023. Click here to see it on the map.
