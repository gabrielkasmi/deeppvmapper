Île-de-France

8 départements

With 12,317,279 inhabitants as of 2021 (1st nationally) spread across 12,060 km² (12th by area), Île-de-France lies in the Paris region, at the heart of northern France. Its economy was estimated at €865,652 million in GDP in 2024 (1st nationally). Paris, Saint-Denis and Boulogne-Billancourt rank among its largest cities.

The latest OpenPVMapper release has identified 354.0 MWp of rooftop PV across 47 850 installations in Île-de-France, making it the 12th région in France by installed capacity. The most equipped départements were Seine-et-Marne, Essonne and Yvelines. Detections span 2017–2024. To explore every detection in Île-de-France, click here to see it on the map.
