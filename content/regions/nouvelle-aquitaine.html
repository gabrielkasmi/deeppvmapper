Nouvelle-Aquitaine

12 départements

Nouvelle-Aquitaine is located in southwestern France, along the Atlantic coast. As of 2021 it counted 6,069,352 inhabitants (the 3rd most populous French région), across 85,185 km² of land (the 1st largest by area). Its GDP stood at €223,433 million in 2024 (provisional), ranking it 3rd nationally. Its largest cities are Bordeaux, Limoges and Poitiers.

162 358 rooftop PV installations totalling 4075.5 MWp have been identified across Nouvelle-Aquitaine by OpenPVMapper — the 1st highest installed capacity among French régions. Gironde, Landes and Lot-et-Garonne lead the région's départements by capacity. These detections span 2017–2024. Click here to see it on the map.
