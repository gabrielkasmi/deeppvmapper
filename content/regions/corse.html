Corse

2 départements

Situated the Mediterranean island region of France, Corse spans 8,725 km² — the 13th largest of France's 13 métropole régions. It was home to 347,597 people as of 2021 (13th most populous), and generated an estimated €13,358 million in GDP in 2024 (ranked 13th). Ajaccio, Bastia and Porto-Vecchio are among its largest cities.

OpenPVMapper's latest release puts Corse at 37.7 MWp of estimated rooftop PV capacity over 4 267 installations — the 13th région in France on that measure. Haute-Corse and Corse-du-Sud account for the largest shares within the région. The detections span 2024–2024; click here to see it on the map.
