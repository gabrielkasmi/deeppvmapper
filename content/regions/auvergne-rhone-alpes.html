Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes

12 départements

Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes is located in east-central France, along the Alps. As of 2021 it counted 8,114,361 inhabitants (the 2nd most populous French région), across 70,895 km² of land (the 3rd largest by area). Its GDP stood at €346,405 million in 2024 (provisional), ranking it 2nd nationally. Its largest cities are Lyon, Saint-Étienne and Villeurbanne.

181 891 rooftop PV installations totalling 1798.3 MWp have been identified across Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes by OpenPVMapper — the 3rd highest installed capacity among French régions. Allier, Cantal and Drôme lead the région's départements by capacity. These detections span 2017–2025. Click here to see it on the map.
