Hauts-de-France

5 départements

With 5,995,292 inhabitants as of 2021 (5th nationally) spread across 31,914 km² (8th by area), Hauts-de-France lies in the far north of France. Its economy was estimated at €197,331 million in GDP in 2024 (7th nationally). Lille, Amiens and Tourcoing rank among its largest cities.

The latest OpenPVMapper release has identified 529.5 MWp of rooftop PV across 75 043 installations in Hauts-de-France, making it the 9th région in France by installed capacity. The most equipped départements were Nord, Pas-de-Calais and Oise. Detections span 2015–2025. To explore every detection in Hauts-de-France, click here to see it on the map.
