Rhône

Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes

Rhône is a département of Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes, with Lyon as its préfecture. As of 2021 it counted 1,914,667 inhabitants. It is known for the Basilica of Notre-Dame de Fourvière in Lyon.

19 616 rooftop PV installations totalling 130.3 MWp have been identified in Rhône by OpenPVMapper, ranking it 47th nationally and 7th out of 12 in Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes. These detections span 2017–2023. 0% of installations in Rhône are confirmed by more than two independent sources. Click here to see it on the map.
