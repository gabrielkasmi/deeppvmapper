Ardèche

Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes

Part of Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes, Ardèche, with Privas as its préfecture counted 334,231 inhabitants as of 2021.

OpenPVMapper's latest release puts Ardèche at 96.0 MWp of estimated rooftop PV capacity over 10 680 installations — 55th in France, 10th out of 12 in Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes. The detections span 2020–2023. 0% of installations in Ardèche are confirmed by more than two independent sources. click here to see it on the map.
