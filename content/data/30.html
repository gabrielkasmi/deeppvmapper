Gard

Occitanie

Gard is a département of Occitanie, with Nîmes as its préfecture. As of 2021 it counted 770,940 inhabitants. It is known for the Pont du Gard.

26 533 rooftop PV installations totalling 295.3 MWp have been identified in Gard by OpenPVMapper, ranking it 10th nationally and 2nd out of 13 in Occitanie. These detections span 2018–2024. 0% of installations in Gard are confirmed by more than two independent sources. Click here to see it on the map.
