Ariège

Occitanie

Ariège is a département of Occitanie, with Foix as its préfecture. As of 2021 it counted 155,722 inhabitants.

5 741 rooftop PV installations totalling 45.0 MWp have been identified in Ariège by OpenPVMapper, ranking it 83rd nationally and 12th out of 13 in Occitanie. These detections span 2019–2025. 0% of installations in Ariège are confirmed by more than two independent sources. Click here to see it on the map.
