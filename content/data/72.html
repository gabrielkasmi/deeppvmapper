Sarthe

Pays de la Loire

Sarthe is a département of Pays de la Loire, with Le Mans as its préfecture. As of 2021 it counted 566,733 inhabitants.

12 802 rooftop PV installations totalling 146.9 MWp have been identified in Sarthe by OpenPVMapper, ranking it 37th nationally and 4th out of 5 in Pays de la Loire. These detections span 2019–2025. 0% of installations in Sarthe are confirmed by more than two independent sources. Click here to see it on the map.
