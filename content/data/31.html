Part of Occitanie, Haute-Garonne, with Toulouse as its préfecture counted 1,471,468 inhabitants as of 2021.

OpenPVMapper's latest release puts Haute-Garonne at 282.2 MWp of estimated rooftop PV capacity over 34 532 installations — 12th in France, 3rd out of 13 in Occitanie. The detections span 2019–2025. 5% of installations in Haute-Garonne are confirmed by more than two independent sources. click here to see it on the map.
