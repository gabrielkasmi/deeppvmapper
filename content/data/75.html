Paris

Île-de-France

Paris is a département of Île-de-France, with Paris as its préfecture. As of 2021 it counted 2,103,778 inhabitants. It is known for the Eiffel Tower and Notre-Dame Cathedral.

2 390 rooftop PV installations totalling 22.5 MWp have been identified in Paris by OpenPVMapper, ranking it 92nd nationally and 6th out of 8 in Île-de-France. These detections span 2018–2024. Click here to see it on the map.
