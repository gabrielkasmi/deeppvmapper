Eure

Normandie

Eure is a département of Normandie, with Évreux as its préfecture. As of 2021 it counted 602,714 inhabitants.

9 526 rooftop PV installations totalling 89.5 MWp have been identified in Eure by OpenPVMapper, ranking it 57th nationally and 1st out of 5 in Normandie. These detections span 2019–2025. 0% of installations in Eure are confirmed by more than two independent sources. Click here to see it on the map.
