Gironde

Nouvelle-Aquitaine

Gironde is a département of Nouvelle-Aquitaine, with Bordeaux as its préfecture. As of 2021 it counted 1,690,493 inhabitants. It is known for the Bordeaux vineyards and the Dune du Pilat.

30 783 rooftop PV installations totalling 1225.0 MWp have been identified in Gironde by OpenPVMapper, ranking it 1st nationally and 1st out of 12 in Nouvelle-Aquitaine. These detections span 2018–2024. 1% of installations in Gironde are confirmed by more than two independent sources. Click here to see it on the map.
