Indre

Centre-Val de Loire

Indre is a département of Centre-Val de Loire, with Châteauroux as its préfecture. As of 2021 it counted 216,069 inhabitants.

4 317 rooftop PV installations totalling 93.1 MWp have been identified in Indre by OpenPVMapper, ranking it 56th nationally and 4th out of 6 in Centre-Val de Loire. These detections span 2020–2023. 1% of installations in Indre are confirmed by more than two independent sources. Click here to see it on the map.
