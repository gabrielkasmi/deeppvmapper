Pas-de-Calais

Hauts-de-France

Pas-de-Calais, in Hauts-de-France, with Arras as its préfecture, counted 1,457,905 inhabitants as of 2021.

The latest OpenPVMapper release has identified 113.2 MWp of rooftop PV across 19 119 installations in Pas-de-Calais — the 51st département in France and 2nd out of 5 within Hauts-de-France by installed capacity. Detections span 2018–2024. 1% of installations in Pas-de-Calais are confirmed by more than two independent sources. To explore every detection in Pas-de-Calais, click here to see it on the map.
