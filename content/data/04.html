Part of Provence-Alpes-Côte d'Azur, Alpes-de-Haute-Provence, with Digne-les-Bains as its préfecture counted 168,054 inhabitants as of 2021.

OpenPVMapper's latest release puts Alpes-de-Haute-Provence at 300.6 MWp of estimated rooftop PV capacity over 6 583 installations — 9th in France, 3rd out of 6 in Provence-Alpes-Côte d'Azur. The detections span 2018–2024. 1% of installations in Alpes-de-Haute-Provence are confirmed by more than two independent sources. click here to see it on the map.
