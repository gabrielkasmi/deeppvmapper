Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes

Haute-Savoie, in Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes, with Annecy as its préfecture, counted 861,158 inhabitants as of 2021. It is known for Mont Blanc.

The latest OpenPVMapper release has identified 74.1 MWp of rooftop PV across 15 830 installations in Haute-Savoie — the 65th département in France and 11th out of 12 within Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes by installed capacity. Detections span 2020–2023. 0% of installations in Haute-Savoie are confirmed by more than two independent sources. To explore every detection in Haute-Savoie, click here to see it on the map.
