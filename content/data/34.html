Hérault

Occitanie

Part of Occitanie, Hérault, with Montpellier as its préfecture counted 1,230,289 inhabitants as of 2021. It is known for the Place de la Comédie in Montpellier.

OpenPVMapper's latest release puts Hérault at 331.2 MWp of estimated rooftop PV capacity over 35 929 installations — 7th in France, 1st out of 13 in Occitanie. The detections span 2018–2024. 1% of installations in Hérault are confirmed by more than two independent sources. click here to see it on the map.
