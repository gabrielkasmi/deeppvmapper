Aveyron

Occitanie

Aveyron is a département of Occitanie, with Rodez as its préfecture. As of 2021 it counted 279,609 inhabitants. It is known for the Millau Viaduct.

10 625 rooftop PV installations totalling 182.6 MWp have been identified in Aveyron by OpenPVMapper, ranking it 27th nationally and 7th out of 13 in Occitanie. These detections span 2019–2022. 6% of installations in Aveyron are confirmed by more than two independent sources. Click here to see it on the map.
