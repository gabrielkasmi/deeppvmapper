Pyrénées-Atlantiques

Nouvelle-Aquitaine

Part of Nouvelle-Aquitaine, Pyrénées-Atlantiques, with Pau as its préfecture counted 706,564 inhabitants as of 2021. It is known for the Basque coast and Béarn Pyrenees.

OpenPVMapper's latest release puts Pyrénées-Atlantiques at 216.1 MWp of estimated rooftop PV capacity over 17 991 installations — 21st in France, 6th out of 12 in Nouvelle-Aquitaine. The detections span 2018–2024. 2% of installations in Pyrénées-Atlantiques are confirmed by more than two independent sources. click here to see it on the map.
