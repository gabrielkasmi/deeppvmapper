Côte-d'Or

Bourgogne-Franche-Comté

Côte-d'Or is a département of Bourgogne-Franche-Comté, with Dijon as its préfecture. As of 2021 it counted 540,100 inhabitants. It is known for the Burgundy vineyards.

7 743 rooftop PV installations totalling 72.1 MWp have been identified in Côte-d'Or by OpenPVMapper, ranking it 68th nationally and 4th out of 8 in Bourgogne-Franche-Comté. These detections span 2020–2023. 0% of installations in Côte-d'Or are confirmed by more than two independent sources. Click here to see it on the map.
