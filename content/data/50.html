Manche

Normandie

Manche, in Normandie, with Saint-Lô as its préfecture, counted 497,522 inhabitants as of 2021. It is known for Mont-Saint-Michel.

The latest OpenPVMapper release has identified 82.6 MWp of rooftop PV across 11 789 installations in Manche — the 63rd département in France and 2nd out of 5 within Normandie by installed capacity. Detections span 2019–2025. 1% of installations in Manche are confirmed by more than two independent sources. To explore every detection in Manche, click here to see it on the map.
