Seine-Maritime

Normandie

Part of Normandie, Seine-Maritime, with Rouen as its préfecture counted 1,260,964 inhabitants as of 2021. It is known for Rouen's Gothic cathedral and the Etretat cliffs.

OpenPVMapper's latest release puts Seine-Maritime at 67.8 MWp of estimated rooftop PV capacity over 11 418 installations — 69th in France, 4th out of 5 in Normandie. The detections span 2019–2025. 0% of installations in Seine-Maritime are confirmed by more than two independent sources. click here to see it on the map.
