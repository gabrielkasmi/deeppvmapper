Creuse

Nouvelle-Aquitaine

Creuse, in Nouvelle-Aquitaine, with Guéret as its préfecture, counted 115,527 inhabitants as of 2021.

The latest OpenPVMapper release has identified 109.4 MWp of rooftop PV across 4 856 installations in Creuse — the 52nd département in France and 12th out of 12 within Nouvelle-Aquitaine by installed capacity. Detections span 2017–2024. 1% of installations in Creuse are confirmed by more than two independent sources. To explore every detection in Creuse, click here to see it on the map.
