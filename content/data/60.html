Hauts-de-France

Oise is a département of Hauts-de-France, with Beauvais as its préfecture. As of 2021 it counted 829,899 inhabitants.

6 347 rooftop PV installations totalling 61.7 MWp have been identified in Oise by OpenPVMapper, ranking it 70th nationally and 3rd out of 5 in Hauts-de-France. These detections span 2018–2024. 0% of installations in Oise are confirmed by more than two independent sources. Click here to see it on the map.
