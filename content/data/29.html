Finistère

Bretagne

Finistère, in Bretagne, with Quimper as its préfecture, counted 933,455 inhabitants as of 2021. It is known for the Pointe du Raz.

The latest OpenPVMapper release has identified 87.8 MWp of rooftop PV across 10 034 installations in Finistère — the 58th département in France and 4th out of 4 within Bretagne by installed capacity. Detections span 2018–2024. 0% of installations in Finistère are confirmed by more than two independent sources. To explore every detection in Finistère, click here to see it on the map.
