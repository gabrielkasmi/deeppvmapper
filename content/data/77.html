Seine-et-Marne, in Île-de-France, with Melun as its préfecture, counted 1,468,108 inhabitants as of 2021.

The latest OpenPVMapper release has identified 135.9 MWp of rooftop PV across 12 098 installations in Seine-et-Marne — the 43rd département in France and 1st out of 8 within Île-de-France by installed capacity.
