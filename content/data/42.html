Loire

Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes

Loire is a département of Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes, with Saint-Étienne as its préfecture. As of 2021 it counted 774,133 inhabitants.

18 042 rooftop PV installations totalling 104.4 MWp have been identified in Loire by OpenPVMapper, ranking it 54th nationally and 9th out of 12 in Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes. These detections span 2019–2025. 0% of installations in Loire are confirmed by more than two independent sources. Click here to see it on the map.
