Charente-Maritime

Nouvelle-Aquitaine

Charente-Maritime, in Nouvelle-Aquitaine, with La Rochelle as its préfecture, counted 672,279 inhabitants as of 2021.

The latest OpenPVMapper release has identified 258.2 MWp of rooftop PV across 19 820 installations in Charente-Maritime — the 14th département in France and 4th out of 12 within Nouvelle-Aquitaine by installed capacity. Detections span 2018–2024. 1% of installations in Charente-Maritime are confirmed by more than two independent sources.
