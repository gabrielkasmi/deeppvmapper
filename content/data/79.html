Deux-Sèvres

Nouvelle-Aquitaine

Part of Nouvelle-Aquitaine, Deux-Sèvres, with Niort as its préfecture counted 375,229 inhabitants as of 2021.

OpenPVMapper's latest release puts Deux-Sèvres at 181.2 MWp of estimated rooftop PV capacity over 12 435 installations — 28th in France, 8th out of 12 in Nouvelle-Aquitaine. The detections span 2020–2023. 1% of installations in Deux-Sèvres are confirmed by more than two independent sources. click here to see it on the map.
