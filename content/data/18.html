Cher

Centre-Val de Loire

Cher is a département of Centre-Val de Loire, with Bourges as its préfecture. As of 2021 it counted 298,660 inhabitants.

6 475 rooftop PV installations totalling 133.7 MWp have been identified in Cher by OpenPVMapper, ranking it 46th nationally and 2nd out of 6 in Centre-Val de Loire. These detections span 2020–2023. 0% of installations in Cher are confirmed by more than two independent sources. Click here to see it on the map.
