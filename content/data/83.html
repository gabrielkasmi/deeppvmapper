Var

Provence-Alpes-Côte d'Azur

Var, in Provence-Alpes-Côte d'Azur, with Toulon as its préfecture, counted 1,119,307 inhabitants as of 2021. It is known for Saint-Tropez.

The latest OpenPVMapper release has identified 335.8 MWp of rooftop PV across 20 963 installations in Var — the 6th département in France and 2nd out of 6 within Provence-Alpes-Côte d'Azur by installed capacity. Detections span 2020–2023. 0% of installations in Var are confirmed by more than two independent sources. To explore every detection in Var, click here to see it on the map.
