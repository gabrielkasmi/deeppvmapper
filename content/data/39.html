Jura

Bourgogne-Franche-Comté

Jura is a département of Bourgogne-Franche-Comté, with Lons-le-Saunier as its préfecture. As of 2021 it counted 257,973 inhabitants.

6 367 rooftop PV installations totalling 83.5 MWp have been identified in Jura by OpenPVMapper, ranking it 62nd nationally and 3rd out of 8 in Bourgogne-Franche-Comté. These detections span 2020–2023. 0% of installations in Jura are confirmed by more than two independent sources. Click here to see it on the map.
