Dordogne

Nouvelle-Aquitaine

Dordogne is a département of Nouvelle-Aquitaine, with Périgueux as its préfecture. As of 2021 it counted 417,614 inhabitants.

11 058 rooftop PV installations totalling 134.4 MWp have been identified in Dordogne by OpenPVMapper, ranking it 45th nationally and 11th out of 12 in Nouvelle-Aquitaine. These detections span 2021–2024. 0% of installations in Dordogne are confirmed by more than two independent sources. Click here to see it on the map.
