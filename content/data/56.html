Morbihan

Bretagne

Morbihan, in Bretagne, with Vannes as its préfecture, counted 783,390 inhabitants as of 2021. It is known for the Carnac megaliths.

The latest OpenPVMapper release has identified 140.4 MWp of rooftop PV across 15 501 installations in Morbihan — the 40th département in France and 2nd out of 4 within Bretagne by installed capacity. Detections span 2019–2024. 0% of installations in Morbihan are confirmed by more than two independent sources. To explore every detection in Morbihan, click here to see it on the map.
