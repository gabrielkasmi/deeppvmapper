Yvelines

Île-de-France

Yvelines is a département of Île-de-France, with Versailles as its préfecture. As of 2021 it counted 1,485,086 inhabitants.

6 065 rooftop PV installations totalling 45.6 MWp have been identified in Yvelines by OpenPVMapper, ranking it 82nd nationally and 3rd out of 8 in Île-de-France. These detections span 2024–2024. 0% of installations in Yvelines are confirmed by more than two independent sources. Click here to see it on the map.
