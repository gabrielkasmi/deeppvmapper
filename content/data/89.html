Bourgogne-Franche-Comté

Yonne, in Bourgogne-Franche-Comté, with Auxerre as its préfecture, counted 332,267 inhabitants as of 2021.

The latest OpenPVMapper release has identified 271.2 MWp of rooftop PV across 3 811 installations in Yonne — the 13th département in France and 1st out of 8 within Bourgogne-Franche-Comté by installed capacity. Detections span 2023–2023. 1% of installations in Yonne are confirmed by more than two independent sources. To explore every detection in Yonne, click here to see it on the map.
