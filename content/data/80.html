Somme

Somme, in Hauts-de-France, with Amiens as its préfecture, counted 565,413 inhabitants as of 2021.

The latest OpenPVMapper release has identified 56.5 MWp of rooftop PV across 7 899 installations in Somme — the 76th département in France and 4th out of 5 within Hauts-de-France by installed capacity. Detections span 2017–2024. 0% of installations in Somme are confirmed by more than two independent sources. To explore every detection in Somme, click here to see it on the map.
