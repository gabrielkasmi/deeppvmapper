Haute-Loire

Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes

Part of Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes, Haute-Loire, with Le Puy-en-Velay as its préfecture counted 228,654 inhabitants as of 2021.

OpenPVMapper's latest release puts Haute-Loire at 145.5 MWp of estimated rooftop PV capacity over 9 866 installations — 38th in France, 5th out of 12 in Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes. The detections span 2019–2025. 3% of installations in Haute-Loire are confirmed by more than two independent sources. click here to see it on the map.
