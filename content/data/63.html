Puy-de-Dôme

Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes

Puy-de-Dôme is a département of Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes, with Clermont-Ferrand as its préfecture. As of 2021 it counted 664,453 inhabitants. It is known for the Puy de Dôme volcano.

16 005 rooftop PV installations totalling 140.3 MWp have been identified in Puy-de-Dôme by OpenPVMapper, ranking it 41st nationally and 6th out of 12 in Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes. These detections span 2019–2025. 0% of installations in Puy-de-Dôme are confirmed by more than two independent sources. Click here to see it on the map.
