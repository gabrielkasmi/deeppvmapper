Pays de la Loire

Part of Pays de la Loire, Maine-et-Loire, with Angers as its préfecture counted 833,776 inhabitants as of 2021.

OpenPVMapper's latest release puts Maine-et-Loire at 204.9 MWp of estimated rooftop PV capacity over 19 704 installations — 22nd in France, 3rd out of 5 in Pays de la Loire. The detections span 2020–2025. 1% of installations in Maine-et-Loire are confirmed by more than two independent sources. click here to see it on the map.
