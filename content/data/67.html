Bas-Rhin

Grand Est

Part of Grand Est, Bas-Rhin, with Strasbourg as its préfecture counted 1,163,810 inhabitants as of 2021. It is known for Strasbourg Cathedral.

OpenPVMapper's latest release puts Bas-Rhin at 167.9 MWp of estimated rooftop PV capacity over 19 620 installations — 34th in France, 3rd out of 10 in Grand Est. The detections span 2018–2024. 0% of installations in Bas-Rhin are confirmed by more than two independent sources. click here to see it on the map.
