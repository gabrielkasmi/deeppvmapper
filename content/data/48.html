Lozère

Occitanie

Lozère is a département of Occitanie, with Mende as its préfecture. As of 2021 it counted 76,486 inhabitants.

2 624 rooftop PV installations totalling 31.8 MWp have been identified in Lozère by OpenPVMapper, ranking it 88th nationally and 13th out of 13 in Occitanie. These detections span 2018–2025. 1% of installations in Lozère are confirmed by more than two independent sources. Click here to see it on the map.
