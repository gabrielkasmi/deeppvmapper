Moselle

Grand Est

Moselle is a département of Grand Est, with Metz as its préfecture. As of 2021 it counted 1,051,309 inhabitants.

17 660 rooftop PV installations totalling 170.3 MWp have been identified in Moselle by OpenPVMapper, ranking it 33rd nationally and 2nd out of 10 in Grand Est. These detections span 2018–2025. 0% of installations in Moselle are confirmed by more than two independent sources. Click here to see it on the map.
