Calvados

Normandie

Calvados, in Normandie, with Caen as its préfecture, counted 709,441 inhabitants as of 2021. It is known for the D-Day landing beaches.

The latest OpenPVMapper release has identified 73.6 MWp of rooftop PV across 7 235 installations in Calvados — the 67th département in France and 3rd out of 5 within Normandie by installed capacity. Detections span 2020–2023. To explore every detection in Calvados, click here to see it on the map.
