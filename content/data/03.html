Allier

Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes

Allier is a département of Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes, with Moulins as its préfecture. As of 2021 it counted 333,298 inhabitants.

10 174 rooftop PV installations totalling 379.3 MWp have been identified in Allier by OpenPVMapper, ranking it 4th nationally and 1st out of 12 in Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes. These detections span 2019–2025. 2% of installations in Allier are confirmed by more than two independent sources. Click here to see it on the map.
