Indre-et-Loire

Centre-Val de Loire

Part of Centre-Val de Loire, Indre-et-Loire, with Tours as its préfecture counted 619,362 inhabitants as of 2021. It is known for the Château de Chenonceau.

OpenPVMapper's latest release puts Indre-et-Loire at 178.6 MWp of estimated rooftop PV capacity over 12 108 installations — 29th in France, 1st out of 6 in Centre-Val de Loire. The detections span 2018–2025. 1% of installations in Indre-et-Loire are confirmed by more than two independent sources. click here to see it on the map.
