Meuse

Grand Est

Part of Grand Est, Meuse, with Bar-le-Duc as its préfecture counted 180,290 inhabitants as of 2021. It is known for the Verdun battlefields.

OpenPVMapper's latest release puts Meuse at 49.9 MWp of estimated rooftop PV capacity over 4 795 installations — 80th in France, 7th out of 10 in Grand Est. The detections span 2018–2025. 0% of installations in Meuse are confirmed by more than two independent sources. click here to see it on the map.
