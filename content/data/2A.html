Corse-du-Sud

Corse

Part of Corse, Corse-du-Sud, with Ajaccio as its préfecture counted 168,306 inhabitants as of 2021. It is known for the Corsican coastline near Ajaccio.

OpenPVMapper's latest release puts Corse-du-Sud at 10.0 MWp of estimated rooftop PV capacity over 2 061 installations — 95th in France, 2nd out of 2 in Corse. The detections span 2024–2024. click here to see it on the map.
