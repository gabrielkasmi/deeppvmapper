Vienne

Nouvelle-Aquitaine

Vienne, in Nouvelle-Aquitaine, with Poitiers as its préfecture, counted 438,897 inhabitants as of 2021. It is known for the Futuroscope park.

The latest OpenPVMapper release has identified 190.1 MWp of rooftop PV across 9 996 installations in Vienne — the 25th département in France and 7th out of 12 within Nouvelle-Aquitaine by installed capacity. Detections span 2020–2023. 2% of installations in Vienne are confirmed by more than two independent sources. To explore every detection in Vienne, click here to see it on the map.
