Bouches-du-Rhône

Provence-Alpes-Côte d'Azur

Part of Provence-Alpes-Côte d'Azur, Bouches-du-Rhône, with Marseille as its préfecture counted 2,087,658 inhabitants as of 2021. It is known for the Calanques and Marseille's Vieux-Port.

OpenPVMapper's latest release puts Bouches-du-Rhône at 386.7 MWp of estimated rooftop PV capacity over 27 690 installations — 3rd in France, 1st out of 6 in Provence-Alpes-Côte d'Azur. The detections span 2020–2023. 0% of installations in Bouches-du-Rhône are confirmed by more than two independent sources. click here to see it on the map.
