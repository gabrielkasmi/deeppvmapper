Nouvelle-Aquitaine

Part of Nouvelle-Aquitaine, Landes, with Mont-de-Marsan as its préfecture counted 433,570 inhabitants as of 2021.

OpenPVMapper's latest release puts Landes at 907.7 MWp of estimated rooftop PV capacity over 18 182 installations — 2nd in France, 2nd out of 12 in Nouvelle-Aquitaine. The detections span 2018–2024. 2% of installations in Landes are confirmed by more than two independent sources. click here to see it on the map.
