Isère

Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes

Isère, in Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes, with Grenoble as its préfecture, counted 1,298,990 inhabitants as of 2021. It is known for the Vercors and Chartreuse massifs.

The latest OpenPVMapper release has identified 173.8 MWp of rooftop PV across 31 737 installations in Isère — the 31st département in France and 4th out of 12 within Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes by installed capacity. Detections span 2018–2024. 1% of installations in Isère are confirmed by more than two independent sources. To explore every detection in Isère, click here to see it on the map.
