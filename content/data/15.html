Cantal

Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes

Cantal is a département of Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes, with Aurillac as its préfecture. As of 2021 it counted 144,196 inhabitants.

6 293 rooftop PV installations totalling 190.1 MWp have been identified in Cantal by OpenPVMapper, ranking it 24th nationally and 2nd out of 12 in Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes. These detections span 2020–2025. 0% of installations in Cantal are confirmed by more than two independent sources. Click here to see it on the map.
