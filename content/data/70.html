Part of Bourgogne-Franche-Comté, Haute-Saône, with Vesoul as its préfecture counted 233,185 inhabitants as of 2021.

OpenPVMapper's latest release puts Haute-Saône at 34.6 MWp of estimated rooftop PV capacity over 5 229 installations — 86th in France, 7th out of 8 in Bourgogne-Franche-Comté. The detections span 2020–2023. 0% of installations in Haute-Saône are confirmed by more than two independent sources. click here to see it on the map.
