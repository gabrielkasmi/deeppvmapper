Seine-Saint-Denis

Île-de-France

Seine-Saint-Denis is a département of Île-de-France, with Bobigny as its préfecture. As of 2021 it counted 1,704,316 inhabitants.

3 989 rooftop PV installations totalling 24.2 MWp have been identified in Seine-Saint-Denis by OpenPVMapper, ranking it 91st nationally and 5th out of 8 in Île-de-France. These detections span 2018–2024. 0% of installations in Seine-Saint-Denis are confirmed by more than two independent sources. Click here to see it on the map.
