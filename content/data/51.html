Marne

Grand Est

Marne is a département of Grand Est, with Châlons-en-Champagne as its préfecture. As of 2021 it counted 563,076 inhabitants.

7 501 rooftop PV installations totalling 84.1 MWp have been identified in Marne by OpenPVMapper, ranking it 61st nationally and 5th out of 10 in Grand Est. These detections span 2019–2025. 1% of installations in Marne are confirmed by more than two independent sources. Click here to see it on the map.
