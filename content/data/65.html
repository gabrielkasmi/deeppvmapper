Hautes-Pyrénées

Occitanie

Hautes-Pyrénées, in Occitanie, with Tarbes as its préfecture, counted 231,349 inhabitants as of 2021.

The latest OpenPVMapper release has identified 48.9 MWp of rooftop PV across 6 123 installations in Hautes-Pyrénées — the 81st département in France and 11th out of 13 within Occitanie by installed capacity. Detections span 2019–2025. 1% of installations in Hautes-Pyrénées are confirmed by more than two independent sources. To explore every detection in Hautes-Pyrénées, click here to see it on the map.
