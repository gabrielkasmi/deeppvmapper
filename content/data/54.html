Meurthe-et-Moselle

Grand Est

Meurthe-et-Moselle is a département of Grand Est, with Nancy as its préfecture. As of 2021 it counted 732,236 inhabitants.

11 202 rooftop PV installations totalling 331.1 MWp have been identified in Meurthe-et-Moselle by OpenPVMapper, ranking it 8th nationally and 1st out of 10 in Grand Est. These detections span 2018–2025. 1% of installations in Meurthe-et-Moselle are confirmed by more than two independent sources. Click here to see it on the map.
