Tarn

Occitanie

Tarn is a département of Occitanie, with Albi as its préfecture. As of 2021 it counted 397,352 inhabitants.

12 782 rooftop PV installations totalling 199.4 MWp have been identified in Tarn by OpenPVMapper, ranking it 23rd nationally and 6th out of 13 in Occitanie. These detections span 2020–2025. Click here to see it on the map.
