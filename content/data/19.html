Nouvelle-Aquitaine

Part of Nouvelle-Aquitaine, Corrèze, with Tulle as its préfecture counted 240,826 inhabitants as of 2021.

OpenPVMapper's latest release puts Corrèze at 175.3 MWp of estimated rooftop PV capacity over 6 023 installations — 30th in France, 9th out of 12 in Nouvelle-Aquitaine. The detections span 2020–2023. 0% of installations in Corrèze are confirmed by more than two independent sources. click here to see it on the map.
