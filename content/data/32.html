Gers, in Occitanie, with Auch as its préfecture, counted 192,645 inhabitants as of 2021.

The latest OpenPVMapper release has identified 245.1 MWp of rooftop PV across 10 840 installations in Gers — the 18th département in France and 5th out of 13 within Occitanie by installed capacity. Detections span 2019–2025. 6% of installations in Gers are confirmed by more than two independent sources. To explore every detection in Gers, click here to see it on the map.
