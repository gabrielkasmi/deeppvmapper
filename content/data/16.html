Charente

Nouvelle-Aquitaine

Part of Nouvelle-Aquitaine, Charente, with Angoulême as its préfecture counted 352,683 inhabitants as of 2021.

OpenPVMapper's latest release puts Charente at 252.4 MWp of estimated rooftop PV capacity over 10 914 installations — 15th in France, 5th out of 12 in Nouvelle-Aquitaine. The detections span 2020–2023. 1% of installations in Charente are confirmed by more than two independent sources. click here to see it on the map.
