Essonne

Île-de-France

Part of Île-de-France, Essonne, with Évry-Courcouronnes as its préfecture counted 1,338,485 inhabitants as of 2021.

OpenPVMapper's latest release puts Essonne at 54.9 MWp of estimated rooftop PV capacity over 10 192 installations — 77th in France, 2nd out of 8 in Île-de-France. The detections span 2018–2024. 0% of installations in Essonne are confirmed by more than two independent sources. click here to see it on the map.
