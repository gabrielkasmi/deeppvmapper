Aude

Occitanie

Aude, in Occitanie, with Carcassonne as its préfecture, counted 379,648 inhabitants as of 2021. It is known for the medieval citadel of Carcassonne.

The latest OpenPVMapper release has identified 158.4 MWp of rooftop PV across 13 906 installations in Aude — the 36th département in France and 9th out of 13 within Occitanie by installed capacity. Detections span 2018–2024. 2% of installations in Aude are confirmed by more than two independent sources. To explore every detection in Aude, click here to see it on the map.
