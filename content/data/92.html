Hauts-de-Seine

Île-de-France

Hauts-de-Seine, in Île-de-France, with Nanterre as its préfecture, counted 1,654,712 inhabitants as of 2021.

The latest OpenPVMapper release has identified 21.1 MWp of rooftop PV across 2 880 installations in Hauts-de-Seine — the 94th département in France and 8th out of 8 within Île-de-France by installed capacity. Detections span 2018–2024. 1% of installations in Hauts-de-Seine are confirmed by more than two independent sources. To explore every detection in Hauts-de-Seine, click here to see it on the map.
