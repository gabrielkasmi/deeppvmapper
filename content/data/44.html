Loire-Atlantique

Pays de la Loire

Loire-Atlantique, in Pays de la Loire, with Nantes as its préfecture, counted 1,487,570 inhabitants as of 2021.

The latest OpenPVMapper release has identified 361.1 MWp of rooftop PV across 25 254 installations in Loire-Atlantique — the 5th département in France and 1st out of 5 within Pays de la Loire by installed capacity. Detections span 2020–2025. 3% of installations in Loire-Atlantique are confirmed by more than two independent sources.
