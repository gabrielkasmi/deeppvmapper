Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes

Part of Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes, Ain, with Bourg-en-Bresse as its préfecture counted 679,344 inhabitants as of 2021.

OpenPVMapper's latest release puts Ain at 117.5 MWp of estimated rooftop PV capacity over 18 219 installations — 50th in France, 8th out of 12 in Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes. The detections span 2018–2024. 0% of installations in Ain are confirmed by more than two independent sources. click here to see it on the map.
