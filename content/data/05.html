Hautes-Alpes, in Provence-Alpes-Côte d'Azur, with Gap as its préfecture, counted 143,467 inhabitants as of 2021.

The latest OpenPVMapper release has identified 117.8 MWp of rooftop PV across 4 985 installations in Hautes-Alpes — the 49th département in France and 5th out of 6 within Provence-Alpes-Côte d'Azur by installed capacity. Detections span 2018–2025. 1% of installations in Hautes-Alpes are confirmed by more than two independent sources. To explore every detection in Hautes-Alpes, click here to see it on the map.
