Alpes-Maritimes

Provence-Alpes-Côte d'Azur

Alpes-Maritimes is a département of Provence-Alpes-Côte d'Azur, with Nice as its préfecture. As of 2021 it counted 1,128,418 inhabitants. It is known for the Promenade des Anglais in Nice.

10 248 rooftop PV installations totalling 57.4 MWp have been identified in Alpes-Maritimes by OpenPVMapper, ranking it 74th nationally and 6th out of 6 in Provence-Alpes-Côte d'Azur. These detections span 2017–2023. 0% of installations in Alpes-Maritimes are confirmed by more than two independent sources. Click here to see it on the map.
