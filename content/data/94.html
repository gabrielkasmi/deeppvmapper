Val-de-Marne

Île-de-France

Part of Île-de-France, Val-de-Marne, with Créteil as its préfecture counted 1,426,929 inhabitants as of 2021.

OpenPVMapper's latest release puts Val-de-Marne at 22.4 MWp of estimated rooftop PV capacity over 3 647 installations — 93rd in France, 7th out of 8 in Île-de-France. The detections span 2018–2024. 1% of installations in Val-de-Marne are confirmed by more than two independent sources. click here to see it on the map.
