Vaucluse

Provence-Alpes-Côte d'Azur

Vaucluse is a département of Provence-Alpes-Côte d'Azur, with Avignon as its préfecture. As of 2021 it counted 572,056 inhabitants. It is known for the Palais des Papes in Avignon.

17 027 rooftop PV installations totalling 232.3 MWp have been identified in Vaucluse by OpenPVMapper, ranking it 20th nationally and 4th out of 6 in Provence-Alpes-Côte d'Azur. These detections span 2021–2024. 2% of installations in Vaucluse are confirmed by more than two independent sources. Click here to see it on the map.
