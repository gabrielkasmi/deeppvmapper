Loir-et-Cher

Centre-Val de Loire

Loir-et-Cher, in Centre-Val de Loire, with Blois as its préfecture, counted 328,543 inhabitants as of 2021. It is known for the Château de Chambord.

The latest OpenPVMapper release has identified 128.9 MWp of rooftop PV across 6 161 installations in Loir-et-Cher — the 48th département in France and 3rd out of 6 within Centre-Val de Loire by installed capacity. Detections span 2018–2023. 1% of installations in Loir-et-Cher are confirmed by more than two independent sources. To explore every detection in Loir-et-Cher, click here to see it on the map.
