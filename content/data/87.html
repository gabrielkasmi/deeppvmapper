Haute-Vienne

Nouvelle-Aquitaine

Haute-Vienne is a département of Nouvelle-Aquitaine, with Limoges as its préfecture. As of 2021 it counted 373,167 inhabitants.

7 769 rooftop PV installations totalling 139.5 MWp have been identified in Haute-Vienne by OpenPVMapper, ranking it 42nd nationally and 10th out of 12 in Nouvelle-Aquitaine. These detections span 2024–2024. 3% of installations in Haute-Vienne are confirmed by more than two independent sources. Click here to see it on the map.
