Eure-et-Loir

Centre-Val de Loire

Part of Centre-Val de Loire, Eure-et-Loir, with Chartres as its préfecture counted 433,129 inhabitants as of 2021. It is known for Chartres Cathedral.

OpenPVMapper's latest release puts Eure-et-Loir at 60.7 MWp of estimated rooftop PV capacity over 5 284 installations — 73rd in France, 6th out of 6 in Centre-Val de Loire. The detections span 2020–2023. 0% of installations in Eure-et-Loir are confirmed by more than two independent sources. click here to see it on the map.
