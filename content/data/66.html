Pyrénées-Orientales

Occitanie

Pyrénées-Orientales is a département of Occitanie, with Perpignan as its préfecture. As of 2021 it counted 496,855 inhabitants. It is known for the Cathar castles of the Pyrénées-Orientales.

19 057 rooftop PV installations totalling 250.9 MWp have been identified in Pyrénées-Orientales by OpenPVMapper, ranking it 16th nationally and 4th out of 13 in Occitanie. These detections span 2021–2024. 0% of installations in Pyrénées-Orientales are confirmed by more than two independent sources. Click here to see it on the map.
