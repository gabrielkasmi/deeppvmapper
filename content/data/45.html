Loiret

Centre-Val de Loire

Loiret is a département of Centre-Val de Loire, with Orléans as its préfecture. As of 2021 it counted 691,268 inhabitants.

7 140 rooftop PV installations totalling 73.8 MWp have been identified in Loiret by OpenPVMapper, ranking it 66th nationally and 5th out of 6 in Centre-Val de Loire. These detections span 2020–2023. 1% of installations in Loiret are confirmed by more than two independent sources. Click here to see it on the map.
