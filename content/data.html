How to use this map

  • Search a city, département, or région by name — it resolves to the exact administrative boundary, not just a rough bounding box.
  • Or click the pencil icon and drag a rectangle on the map to browse a custom area.
  • Detections show as clusters. Zoom in, or click a marker, to reveal that installation's exact rooftop outline and a popup with its details (surface, capacity, first seen). Click anywhere else on the map to go back to the cluster view.
  • Click the eraser icon to clear the current selection.
  • Click the gear icon to filter the selection by power, year, or source, or to hide detections flagged as false positives — the sliders only let you pick values actually present in the data.
  • Download the current (filtered) selection as GeoJSON or CSV with the button at the bottom of the map.
  • Switch between the street map and satellite imagery with the toggle at the top right.
  • Spotted a mistake? Click an installation to open its popup, then use False positive if it isn't really a PV system, or Redraw to trace a corrected outline over the dimmed original. Both apply to your view immediately and are submitted for review.
  • Missing an installation entirely? Click Add missing installation at the bottom of the map, then trace its outline directly on the map (click to place each corner, click the first point again to close the shape).
  • For any edit, you can add an optional comment and estimated surface/capacity before submitting — nothing is deleted from the official dataset on your say-so alone; edits are reviewed before they're merged in.
