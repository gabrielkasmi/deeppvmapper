How to play

  • The ✛ marker shows where to look — the question is "is there a solar installation here", not whether an outline is drawn perfectly.
  • → swipe right, click ✓, or right arrow — it's a solar panel
  • ← swipe left, click ✕, or left arrow — not a PV system
  • ↓ swipe down, click ↓ Skip, or down arrow — come back to this one later (broken image, obstructed view…), doesn't count as a vote and isn't one of your counters below
  • ? click ? Unsure — you have a hunch but you're not sure — ambiguous, with an optional comment, this DOES count as a vote
  • ↩ undoes your last action, while the button is still shown
